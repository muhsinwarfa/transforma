# Your Review — A Guide for Team Members

Welcome to Transforma at Riverbend Health. This guide walks you through your own
performance review, from nominating the people who will give feedback to reading and
acknowledging your final review. Follow the sections in order, or jump to whatever you
need right now.

A quick word on how it works: the system **synthesizes your real self-review and your
peers' real feedback into a draft narrative**. Your Manager reviews that narrative, edits
it, and writes your rating themselves. Peer feedback is always confidential and is never
attributed to anyone.

---

## Nominate your peer reviewers

*What this is for: choosing the colleagues you'd like to give feedback on your work this
cycle.*

1. Open your review and go to the **Nominate peers** step.
2. Search for colleagues by name and select the people you'd like to nominate.
3. Aim for **at least 3** peers. Peer feedback is confidential and is only used in your
   draft when 3 or more peers respond.
4. When your list is ready, select **Submit**.

![The peer nomination screen with a search box and a list of selected colleagues.](images/S3-nominate.png)

If you pick fewer than 3 peers, you'll see a heads-up before submitting. It explains that,
below 3 responses, the anonymity floor isn't met, so your draft may be built from your
**self-review alone**. You can **Submit anyway** or **Go back** to add more peers.

![A warning shown when fewer than three peers are selected, explaining the review may rely on the self-review alone.](images/S3-below-floor.png)

After you submit, your nominations go to your Manager for approval. You'll see them marked
as **pending** while you wait.

![The nominations shown in a pending state, awaiting Manager approval.](images/S3-pending.png)

If your Manager asks for a change, you'll see their note exactly as they wrote it. Read it,
adjust your list, and **resubmit**.

![A change request from the Manager showing their note, with the option to edit and resubmit nominations.](images/S3-changes.png)

## Write and submit your self-review

*What this is for: telling your own story of the cycle in your words.*

1. Open the **Self-review** step and answer each question.
2. Your answers **save automatically as you go** — you can leave and come back without
   losing anything.
3. When you're happy with your answers, select **Submit**.
4. Submitting locks your answers as read-only; you won't be able to change them afterward.

![The self-review with answers being filled in; a note confirms answers save as you go.](images/S4-self-progress.png)

After you submit, you'll see a short explainer of what happens next. Your self-review and
your peers' feedback are synthesized into a draft narrative for your Manager. **Drafts stay
private until your review is final** — you won't see any in-progress content.

![The submitted confirmation explaining what happens next and that drafts stay private until final.](images/S4-self-submitted.png)

## Track where your review is

*What this is for: seeing, at a glance, how far along your review is.*

1. Open your review's **Status** view.
2. The timeline shows each step: what's **done**, what's happening **Now**, and what's still
   **upcoming**, with dates.
3. The **Now** marker points to the current step.
4. A privacy note reminds you that draft content stays private until your review is final.

![A status timeline with completed, current ('Now'), and upcoming steps, plus a privacy note.](images/S5-status.png)

---

## Read and acknowledge your final review

*What this is for: reading your finished review and confirming you've seen it.*

1. When your review is ready, open it. You'll see your **rating**, the full **narrative**,
   and a concrete **acknowledge-by date**.
2. Read it carefully. The rating is written by your Manager.

![The finished review showing the rating, the narrative, and an acknowledge-by date.](images/S6-ready.png)

3. To acknowledge, open the acknowledgement form. You'll be warned first: **acknowledging
   locks the review permanently** and it can't be changed afterward.
4. You may add an **optional written response** — it's kept on record with your review.
5. Select **Acknowledge** to confirm.

![The acknowledgement form, warning that acknowledging locks the review and offering an optional written response.](images/S6-ack-form.png)

Once acknowledged, your review is **locked**. The locked view shows it's complete and
permanent.

![The locked review after acknowledgement, marked complete and permanent.](images/S6-locked-ack.png)

---

## Disagree on the record

*What this is for: recording a written response when you don't agree with your review.*

1. From your final review, choose to **flag** it instead of simply acknowledging.
2. A **written reason is required** — explain, in your words, where and why you disagree.
3. Submit your response.

![The flag form, which requires a written reason for disagreeing with the review.](images/S7-flag-form.png)

Your review still **locks permanently**, but your response and the date you filed it are
**stored with the review** on the record.

![The locked, flagged review showing your written response and the date it was stored.](images/S7-locked-flagged.png)

---

## When there's nothing to do yet

*What this is for: knowing it's fine when there's no action waiting on you.*

If there's no review or task assigned to you right now, you'll see a calm, clear empty
state. There's nothing you need to do — check back when a new cycle opens or when your
Manager moves your review forward.

![A calm empty state shown when there's no review or task waiting for you.](images/S8-no-review.png)

---

**If something looks off** — a step seems stuck, a date looks wrong, or you can't find your
review — reach out to your People Ops team. They can check on your review and help you get
unstuck.
